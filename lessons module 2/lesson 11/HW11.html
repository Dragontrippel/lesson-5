My Fitness Routine Cards

The cards shown below are my fitness routine for every day, and how long they take.

Warmup

Start the day with a simple jog, arm circles and high knees.

Time: 10 minutes

First workout

Do 10 squats, 8 push-ups, 15 jumping jacks a 20 second plank and 10 lunges per leg and then repeat this 3x.

Time: 20 minutes

Speed workout

Sprinting, high knees again, skater jumps and some burpees.

Time: 15 minutes

Sport workout

Try squats, mountain climbers, push-ups and a plank.

Time: 15 minutes

Evening Walk

Go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind.

Time: 25 minutes

Rest and Hydrate

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout.

Time: All day

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency.
