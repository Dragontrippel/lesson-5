Information about the Sports

Hockey --) Hockey is a fast-paced sport where teams use curved sticks to hit a puck or ball into the opponent's net.
The goal is simply to outscore the other team.

Football --) Football is a global sport where two 11-player teams compete to score by kicking a round ball into the opponent's goal.
Its fast-paced action and massive tournaments like the FIFA World Cup unite billions of fans worldwide.

Basketball --) Basketball is a fast-paced game where two five-player teams score points by shooting a ball through an elevated hoop.
Packed with quick dribbling and high-flying dunks, the sport dominates global pop culture and the NBA stage.

Badminton --) Badminton is a lightning-fast racket sport where two or four players hit a feathered shuttlecock back and forth over a high net.
Requiring quick reflexes and agile footwork, players score points by landing the shuttlecock inside the opponent's court boundaries.

Tennis --) Tennis is a popular racket sport played individually or in pairs on a rectangular court divided by a low net.
Players score points by using strung rackets to strike a yellow felt ball over the net so the opponent cannot return it.

Ping Pong --) Ping pong, also known as table tennis, is a fast-paced sport played on a flat table divided by a small center net.
Players use small solid paddles to hit a lightweight plastic ball back and forth, scoring points when the opponent fails to return it.
